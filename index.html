1. Choose a decision problem

Load an example, or edit the alternatives, states of nature, payoffs and probabilities below. All results recalculate automatically.

2. Alternatives and states of nature

Alternatives are the actions you can choose. States of nature are future situations outside your control. Payoffs are assumed to be profits or benefits, so larger values are better.

3. Decision criteria

Maximax is optimistic; Maximin is cautious; Minimax Regret minimizes the worst opportunity loss; EMV/Bayes uses the state probabilities.

4. Staged filtering

The default sequence is Maximin → Minimax Regret → EMV/Bayes. Each stage keeps the alternative(s) with the best score among those that survived the previous stage. Ties are retained. Change the order if your class uses a different sequence.

5. Flow diagram

This diagram updates with the filtering stages and shows the final recommended alternative(s).
